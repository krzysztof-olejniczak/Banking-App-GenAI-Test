**2. Build Tool:** Maven

**3. Script/File Name:** pom.xml

**4. Detailed Documentation:**

* **Project Metadata:**
    -  `groupId`: `io.genai.banking.app.test` - Identifies the project's group within a larger organizational structure.
    -  `artifactId`: `Banking-App-GenAI-Test` - Unique identifier for the project artifact within its group.
    -  `version`: `1.0-SNAPSHOT` - Current version of the project, indicating it's still under development.
